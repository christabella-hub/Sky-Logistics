About Sky Logistics Rwanda

Customs expertise, built for how trade actually moves through Rwanda

Sky Logistics Rwanda specialises in shipment tax calculation, customs clearance, import and export documentation, tax consultation, and shipment compliance. We work alongside individuals, importers, exporters, and businesses of every size to keep goods moving through Rwandan customs without delay or costly error.

Every file we handle is checked against current Rwanda Revenue Authority requirements before it's submitted — so duties are calculated correctly the first time, and clearance isn't held up by paperwork that could have been right from the start.

How It Works

From shipment details to clearance, in six steps

1

Submit your shipment details

Tell us what's shipping, where it's coming from, and where it's going — through the quote form or directly with our team.

2

We review your documents

Invoices, packing lists, and permits are checked against RRA requirements before anything is filed.

3

We calculate applicable taxes and duties

Import duty, VAT, and any levies are calculated against the correct tariff classification, and shared with you.

4

We prepare customs documentation

Declarations and supporting paperwork are compiled to the standard customs and border agents expect.

5

We guide you through payment and clearance

You'll know exactly what's owed and when, with support through the RRA payment process.

6

Your shipment is cleared

Goods are released and documentation is handed over, ready for pickup or onward delivery.
